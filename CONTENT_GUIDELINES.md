# CONTENT_GUIDELINES.md — Religious Accuracy & Sources

## 1. Prime Directive: Zero Tolerance for Fabrication
Islamic religious content forms the spiritual foundation of this application. Under no circumstances may an AI or developer generate, hallucinate, alter, or synthesize:
- Quranic verses (آيات القرآن الكريم).
- Prophetic Hadiths (الأحاديث النبوية الشريفة).
- Chains of transmission (الأسانيد والرواة).
- Source references (تخريج الأحاديث وأرقامها).
- Rulings of authenticity (أحكام المحدثين).

## 2. Authorized Primary Sources
1. **The Holy Quran** (حفص عن عاصم، بالرسم العثماني).
2. **Sahih al-Bukhari** (صحيح البخاري).
3. **Sahih Muslim** (صحيح مسلم).
4. **The Sunan**: Abu Dawud, At-Tirmidhi, An-Nasa'i, Ibn Majah.
5. Recognized scholarly verification databases (Dorar.net, Sunnah.com official reference).

## 3. Classification of Acts
In both Wudu and Salah, every step must be clearly labeled:
- **فرض / واجب (Obligatory)**: What is mandatory; without it, the worship is invalid.
- **سنة مؤكدة / سنة (Sunnah)**: The confirmed practice of the Prophet ﷺ which brings immense reward, but omission does not invalidate the act.
- **مستحب / أدب (Recommended / Etiquette)**: Desirable manners and virtues.

## 4. Handling Scholarly Differences
When established schools of jurisprudence (Madhahib) hold differing views on non-core details (e.g., placing hands below or above navel, touching head once or thrice):
- Present the most widely agreed upon sunnah clearly.
- Note valid differences respectfully without confusing the beginner learner.
- Never present an opinion as unanimous when authentic scholars differ.

## 5. Review & Verification Workflow
- All seed data is rigorously reviewed against authenticated Hadith texts with full vowelization (Tashkeel) where necessary for correct recitation (e.g., Tashahhud, Surah Al-Fatihah, Adhkar of Ruku and Sujud).
- Content not yet verified by a qualified reviewer is marked `pending_review` and kept in `draft` mode.
